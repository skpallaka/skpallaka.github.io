Quant Backtester

A from-scratch backtesting engine with a pluggable strategy interface and five strategies (moving average crossover, RSI, momentum, mean reversion, logistic regression). Performance is judged with Sharpe ratio, max drawdown and bootstrap confidence intervals, with no-lookahead execution and transaction costs.

Runs behind a Postgres-backed Flask API with async jobs through Celery and Redis, containerized with Docker and deployed locally on Kubernetes. Includes a PySpark analysis job and a 49-test pytest suite.

Alphabet P&L Forecast

Forecasts Alphabet's quarterly revenue by segment and operating income from SEC EDGAR filings, with a live-formula Excel model and ETS / SARIMA models compared in a rolling-origin backtest.

Finding: a simple year-over-year growth rule matched ETS and SARIMA at roughly 4–7% error per segment, and Cloud growth is the biggest swing factor in operating income.
