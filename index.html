Featured

Backtesting Engine & Async Job API

A from-scratch engine plus a Flask/Celery job API, run on Kubernetes, that tests whether trading strategies actually beat noise.

Finding: of 55 strategy/stock combinations tested on held-out data, only 4 had returns above zero with 95% confidence, and all 4 were tech or tech-heavy (QQQ, MSFT, NVDA). The strongest strategy's edge disappeared on non-tech stocks. The engine is built to tell a real edge from noise, and here it showed this one doesn't generalize.

A from-scratch engine with a pluggable strategy interface and five strategies (moving average crossover, RSI, momentum, mean reversion, logistic regression), no-lookahead execution, transaction costs, and Sharpe, max drawdown and bootstrap confidence intervals.

Runs behind a Postgres-backed Flask API with async jobs through Celery and Redis, containerized with Docker and orchestrated on Kubernetes with load-balanced, self-healing replicas (tested on a local cluster). Includes a PySpark job across 34 tickers and a 49-test pytest suite running in CI.

Alphabet P&L Forecast

Forecasts Alphabet's quarterly revenue by segment and operating income from SEC EDGAR filings, with a live-formula Excel model and ETS / SARIMA models compared in a rolling-origin backtest.

Finding: a simple year-over-year growth rule matched ETS and SARIMA at roughly 4–7% error per segment, and Cloud growth is the biggest swing factor in operating income.

Wearable Exercise Tracker

An end-to-end sensing pipeline: Arduino/C++ firmware streams 6-axis IMU data over Bluetooth Low Energy into a Python client, which counts exercise reps in real time by auto-selecting the dominant motion channel and smoothing the signal.

Includes a labeled data-collection protocol (per-exercise, per-subject sessions logged to CSV) for building a multi-channel IMU dataset.
